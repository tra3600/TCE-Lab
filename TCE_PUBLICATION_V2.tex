\begin{abstract}
We extend the Chrono-Energetic Theory (TCE) where physical time $T$ is not a primitive parameter but an emergent quantity $T(E) = \int dE / E_L$, with $E_L$ the lapse energy constant. From $dE = T_{thermo} dS$ we derive $dt = T_{thermo} dS / E_L$. This links entropy production to time flow.

We explore: (1) Biology: high entropy production generates more local emergent time. Cellular senescence is reinterpreted as acceleration of local clock. Prediction: caloric restriction, hibernation, cryobiosis slow $dt$. (2) Astronautics: $E_{avail}=E_{tot}-E_{locked}$ and $\Gamma_{TCE}=E_{avail}(r)/E_{avail}(\infty)$. A controlled negative energy bubble (scalar confinement) can reduce $E_{avail}$ without relativistic velocity, producing artificial time dilation for interstellar travel.

Three falsifiable experiments are proposed with atomic clocks near high/low entropy reactors and cell cultures.
\end{abstract}
